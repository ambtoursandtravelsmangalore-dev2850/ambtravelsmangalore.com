Coastal Karnataka Tour from Mangalore

Beaches, temples, heritage and scenic coastal roads

Coastal Karnataka offers a beautiful combination of Arabian Sea beaches, ancient temples, fishing villages, backwaters, islands and historic destinations. AMB Tours & Travels provides customized Coastal Karnataka tour packages from Mangalore for families, couples, friends and groups.


Depending on your travel plan, the tour can include destinations such as Udupi, Malpe, Kaup, Kundapura, Maravanthe, Murudeshwar and Gokarna. The itinerary can be customized according to your available days, preferred attractions and vehicle requirement.

Popular Coastal Karnataka Destinations

Discover some of the most popular coastal attractions

Mangalore

Begin your coastal journey from Mangalore and explore its beaches, temples, churches, markets and coastal culture.

Udupi

Visit the famous Sri Krishna Temple and explore the coastal surroundings and traditional cuisine of Udupi.

Malpe Beach

Enjoy the sandy coastline and scenic surroundings of Malpe, a popular beach destination near Udupi.

St. Mary's Island

Explore the distinctive coastal scenery and natural rock formations of St. Mary's Island.

Kaup Beach

Visit Kaup Beach and its famous lighthouse while enjoying the Arabian Sea views.

Maravanthe

Experience the scenic coastal road where the Arabian Sea and the Souparnika River create a unique landscape.

Murudeshwar

Visit the famous Murudeshwar Temple, Shiva statue, Raja Gopuram and the beach beside the Arabian Sea.

Gokarna

Explore the historic temple town and popular beaches including Om Beach and Kudle Beach.
