Popular Places to Visit in Goa

Explore beaches, heritage attractions and scenic locations

Baga Beach

A popular North Goa beach known for its coastal atmosphere, restaurants and nearby attractions.

Calangute Beach

One of Goa's well-known beaches and a popular stop for visitors exploring North Goa.

Anjuna Beach

Visit Anjuna for its coastal scenery, relaxed atmosphere and nearby local attractions.

Fort Aguada

Explore this historic Portuguese-era fort and enjoy views towards the Arabian Sea.

Basilica of Bom Jesus

Visit the historic basilica in Old Goa and explore the surrounding heritage area.

Panjim

Explore Goa's capital city, including its heritage streets, riverfront and local attractions.

Old Goa

Discover historic churches and important heritage attractions in the Old Goa area.

South Goa Beaches

Explore quieter coastal destinations and scenic beaches in South Goa according to your itinerary.
