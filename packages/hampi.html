Hampi Tour from Mangalore

Experience Karnataka's historic Vijayanagara heritage

Hampi is one of Karnataka's most famous heritage destinations, known for its ancient temples, massive stone structures, monuments and remarkable landscapes. A Hampi trip from Mangalore is ideal for families, friends, couples and groups interested in history, architecture and culture.


AMB Tours & Travels provides private taxi services from Mangalore to Hampi with comfortable vehicle options including Sedan, MPV, SUV, Tempo Traveller, Urbania and Mini Bus. Tour plans can be customized according to your travel dates, group size and sightseeing requirements.

Popular Places to Visit in Hampi

Explore the famous heritage attractions of Hampi

Virupaksha Temple

Visit one of Hampi's most prominent historic temples and experience its impressive architecture.

Vijaya Vittala Temple

Explore the famous temple complex known for its historic architecture and iconic stone structures.

Stone Chariot

See Hampi's iconic stone chariot, one of the most recognized monuments in the historic site.

Lotus Mahal

Discover the elegant historic structure located within the Zenana Enclosure.

Elephant Stables

Visit the impressive historic row of structures associated with the royal era of Hampi.

Hampi Bazaar

Explore the historic market area and experience the atmosphere surrounding Hampi's heritage monuments.
