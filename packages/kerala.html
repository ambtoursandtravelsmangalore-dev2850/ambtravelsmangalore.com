Kerala Tour from Mangalore

Discover the natural beauty and cultural attractions of Kerala

Kerala offers a wide variety of travel experiences, from beautiful beaches and historic towns to misty hill stations, waterfalls, wildlife destinations and cultural attractions. AMB Tours & Travels provides private taxi tours from Mangalore to Kerala for families, couples and groups.


Depending on your available time, the trip can focus on nearby destinations such as Kannur, Bekal and Wayanad, or extend further into Kerala towards Kozhikode, Kochi, Munnar and Thekkady.


Vehicle options include Sedan, MPV, SUV, Tempo Traveller, Urbania and Mini Bus.
