Mysore, also known as the City of Palaces, is one of Karnataka's most popular tourist destinations. From the magnificent Mysore Palace to Chamundi Hills and Brindavan Gardens, the city offers attractions for families, couples and groups.


AMB Tours & Travels provides private taxi services from Mangalore to Mysore for sightseeing and customized tour packages. Choose from Sedan, MPV, SUV, Tempo Traveller, Urbania and Mini Bus depending on your group size.

Popular Places to Visit in Mysore

Explore the famous attractions of Mysore

Mysore Palace

Visit the magnificent royal palace and experience the architectural heritage of Mysore.

Chamundi Hills

Enjoy panoramic views of Mysore and visit the famous Chamundeshwari Temple.

Brindavan Gardens

Explore the beautiful landscaped gardens and enjoy the famous musical fountain.

St. Philomena's Church

See one of Mysore's prominent historic churches known for its impressive Gothic architecture.

Mysore Zoo

A popular attraction for families and visitors interested in wildlife and nature.

Karanji Lake

Enjoy a peaceful nature experience close to the city with scenic surroundings.
